# Ticket02 native execution originals

[provenance.json](provenance.json) binds original paths, permanent archive paths and verbatim SHA256. `original/` is the first02 overlay; `followup/` is its independently acknowledged takeover/fix overlay. Each log keeps its actual input/source/status/cutoff. A later passing group does not reclassify an earlier failing, mechanically invalid or deliberately stopped run. The [execution narrative](../ticket-02-evidence.md) distinguishes business red, mechanical Close/clock/confirmation controls, compile/fixture failures and native unknown.

The final170-name manifest has five normal and five race groups. Wrong-cwd `materialized-race-other.log` and accidentally whole-package `materialized-race-content-a.log` are excluded; their distinct final logs qualify the eventual complete groups. Original timeout and exact-owner SIGTERM boundaries are retained alongside actual ledger observations. Per-process exit/group absence never supplies individual Store/Object/Rows/File Close or rollback ACK. Audits only query exact registered identities; no prefix/PID/time cleanup occurs.

Captured WIP/diagnostic/fault sources remain inert `.go.txt` evidence; original content hashes are unchanged. Their qualification files retain whether the snapshot was captured before compilation, after execution or reconstructed from known edits. They are not claimed to equal an unrelated earlier HEAD. Raw JSON-plus-native-text audit records are `.raw`; normalized machine JSON and actual completion metadata are separate. Local archive attributes preserve original whitespace and final blank lines. No credentials or binary executable/cache artifacts are included.
